The **Ramakoti Yagna** is a devotional writing initiative by *DevaBhasha: The Sanskrit Society of IITM BS*. We collectively aim to write **1 crore (10 million) "Rama" Nama chits** as an offering to Lord Rama. Below are the key highlights and details about the initiative:

**What is Ramakoti Yagna?**

- **"Ramakoti":** Refers to writing "Rama" 10 million times.

- **"Yagna":** A sacred act of devotion and collective offering.

- **Goal:** To offer the written Rama chits as a garland at Tirumala Temple, or another Vishnu temple.

- **Purpose:**

  - Foster spiritual growth through dedication.
  - Unite us in a selfless, collective act of devotion.

1. **Commitment:**

   - Write "Rama" repeatedly in a dedicated long-sized notebook.
   - Use any language you prefer: Sanskrit, English, Tamil, Hindi, etc.
   - Set a weekly writing target based on your pace (e.g., 500, 1000 names).

2. **Tracking Progress:**

   - Update your contributions weekly via progress tracker g-form that will be shared.
   - Stay motivated with weekly progress updates.
   - Join us on monthly co-writing sessions to write together

3. **Final Offering:**

   - Submit notebooks via a central postal address.
   - Volunteers will prepare the pages into folded chits, which will be garlanded.

**Volunteer Opportunities**

- **Progress Tracking:** Ensure contributions are logged accurately and send progress updates.
- **Communication:** Send motivational reminders and handle participant queries.
- **Collection Coordination:** Manage notebook collection and prepare chits for the garland.

**Key Dates**

- **Start Date:** December 16, 2024 (beginning of Margazhi month).
- **Target Completion:** March 2025.
