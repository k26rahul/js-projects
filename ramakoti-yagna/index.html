What is Ramakoti Yagna?

"Ramakoti" refers to writing "Rama" 10 million times, and "Yagna" is a sacred act of devotion and collective offering.

Our Goal

To offer the written Rama chits as a garland at Tirumala Temple or another Vishnu temple.

Key Dates

Start Date: December 16, 2024 (beginning of Margazhi month)

Target Completion: March 2025
