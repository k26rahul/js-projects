**Ramakoti Yagna - Write Rama’s Name 10 Million Times!**

We are delighted to announce the **Ramakoti Yagna**, a devotional writing initiative by _DevaBhasha: The Sanskrit Society of IITM BS_. Together, we aim to offer **1 crore (10 million) "Rama" Nama chits** as a collective offering to Lord Rama.

**What is Ramakoti Yagna?**

- **"Ramakoti":** Writing "Rama" 10 million times.
- **Goal:** Offer written Rama chits at Tirumala Temple or a Vishnu temple.
- **Purpose:** Foster spiritual growth and unity through devotion.

**How to Participate?**

1. **Write "Rama" Names:**

   - Use any language: Sanskrit, English, Hindi, Tamil, etc.
   - Maintain a weekly target (e.g., 500, 1000 names).

2. **Track Your Progress:**

   - Update your contributions weekly via a progress tracker.
   - Join monthly co-writing sessions for collective motivation.

3. **Final Submission:**
   - Send completed notebooks to a central address.
   - Volunteers will prepare chits for garland offerings.

**Volunteer Opportunities**

- **Progress Tracking:** Log contributions and share updates.
- **Communication:** Motivate participants and handle queries.
- **Collection Coordination:** Manage notebooks and chit preparation.

**Key Dates**

- **Start Date:** December 16, 2024 (Margazhi month begins).
- **Target Completion:** March 2025.
